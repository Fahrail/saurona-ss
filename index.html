02

Prefixes And Roles

Chat prefixes are hex-colored and ordered so playstyle appears first, then the main identity prefix, then faction leader when applicable.

Chat Order [H]/[M] - staff, faction, or Wanderer - [L] - name.
Tab List Only the main prefix shows. Staff can be kept near the top.
Default New players receive the Wanderer prefix until they join a faction.
/prefixcheck <player> /staff add <player> <role> /staff remove <player>

Playstyles

Haven is the default. Mortal is a permanent opt-in using the Mortal oath confirmation flow.

Haven [H]

PvP protected against players, keep inventory enabled, and excluded from Mortal-only bounty play.

Mortal [M]

PvP enabled with other Mortals, inventory drops on death, and eligible for bounties.

/playstyle /mortal /mortal confirm /mortal reject /playstyle set <player> <haven|mortal>

05

Backpacks, Playtime, And AFK

Players start with a 1-row backpack and can progress up to 6 rows through time-played rewards. AFK time does not count toward playtime rewards.

  • Rewards happen every 10 hours up to 100 hours, every 25 hours from 100 to 200, then every 50 hours after 200.
  • /craft and /ct open a normal crafting table screen after the reward unlock, and staff can use them freely.
  • AFK state is tracked by inactivity and shown in game.
/backpack /bp /backpack tier /backpack upgrade /backpack downgrade /timedrewards /playtime /craft /ct

Starter Items And Spawn Protection

The mod can require linked players to keep the Middle-earth map and Starlight Phial before leaving spawn, and can protect spawn from PvP or block interaction based on config.

  • middle-earth:middle_earth_map
  • middle-earth:starlight_phial

09

Discord And Account Linking

The server-side mod exposes the Minecraft half of Discord linking and supports staff verification for edge cases. The bot handles Discord commands, role sync, bridge messages, and Discord-side automation.

/link <code> /link verify <player>
  • Unlinked players can be restricted until linked or staff verified.
  • Haven, Mortal, Wanderer, and faction role sync are handled through the bot integration.
  • Faction changes can remove the Wanderer Discord role.

Extra Checks

The alt checker records and compares connection data so staff can inspect possible alternate accounts.

/possiblealts <player>
